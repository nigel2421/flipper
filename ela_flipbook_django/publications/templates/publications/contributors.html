Terms and Conditions for Contributors

  • Content Requirements: Contributors must submit original, high-quality articles that align with the magazine\'s themes of business, innovation, and leadership in Africa. Content must not be published elsewhere.
  • Submission Guidelines: Articles should be well-researched, engaging, and meet our editorial standards for clarity, accuracy, and style. Word count and formatting guidelines will be provided upon pitch acceptance.
  • Compensation: Please note that contributors will NOT receive monetary compensation or any other financial benefits for articles accepted and published by Business Matters Africa Magazine. Contribution is on a voluntary basis.
  • Rights: By submitting your content, you grant Business Matters Africa Magazine exclusive first publication rights and the right to distribute the content across our digital platforms and in print.
  • Byline: Contributors must provide their real, legal names for use as bylines on all published articles. Pseudonyms are not permitted to maintain transparency and credibility.
  • Deadline: Contributors are expected to meet all agreed-upon deadlines for initial submission and any subsequent revisions. Timeliness is crucial to our publication schedule.
  • Communication: Contributors agree to work collaboratively with our editorial team, be receptive to feedback, and make necessary revisions to prepare the article for publication.
