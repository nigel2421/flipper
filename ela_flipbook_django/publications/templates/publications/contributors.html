Terms for Contributors

  • Content Requirements: Contributors submit original, high-quality articles aligning with the magazine's themes and tone.
  • Submission Guidelines: Articles should be well-researched, engaging, and meet our editorial standards.
  • Compensation: Contributors will NOT receive monetary compensation or other benefits based on article acceptance and publication.
  • Rights: By submitting content, contributors grant Business Matters Africa Magazine publication rights.
  • Byline: Contributors must provide their real names for use as bylines for published articles.
  • Deadline: Contributors should meet agreed-upon deadlines for submission and revisions.
  • Communication: Contributors will work with our editorial team for feedback and revisions.
