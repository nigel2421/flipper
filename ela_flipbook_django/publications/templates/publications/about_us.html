About Us

Where serious African business conversations happen.

Our History

Business Matters Africa is an independent, premium business magazine that delivers in-depth, contextually grounded insights for Africa's high-calibre leaders, founders, executives, investors, and family-business principals. Launched in November 2025, we deliberately position ourselves against the tide of superficial business content: no clickbait lists, no recycled generic advice, no obsession with viral trends. Instead, every edition focuses on depth — exploring the psychology of power, succession in family enterprises, contrarian strategies that win in volatile markets, wealth discipline, the economics of education, and long-term legacy building — all written with explicit reference to African realities, constraints, opportunities, and cultural nuances.

We publish in two primary formats:

  • Premium limited-edition print-collectible hard copies with elegant, high-quality design (pre-order model)
  • High-fidelity digital editions - optimised for thoughtful, extended reading
  • Supported by a weekly newsletter and an expanding digital archive at businessmatters.co.ke

Our mission is to become the trusted, desk-reference publication for serious African business builders who value signal over noise.

Our Vision

To be recognised across the continent and diaspora as Africa's premier business publication — the one that discerning executives keep, cite, recommend, and pass on to the next generation of founders and decision-makers; the benchmark for thoughtful, substantive business media on the continent.

Our Purpose

To equip Africa's most ambitious and reflective leaders with intellectual clarity, strategic courage, and Africa-centric perspectives that enable them to build sustainable enterprises, protect generational wealth, navigate complexity, and lead with intention in high-stakes environments.

Our Mission

  • Curate and publish rigorous, practitioner-informed content that rewards deep engagement and repeated reading
  • Foster a selective, high-signal community of real operators who share experience-grounded lessons rather than commentary
  • Integrate global strategic excellence with African market psychology, operational realities, cultural dynamics, and structural opportunities
  • Champion long-term orientation, intellectual honesty, disciplined ambition, and professional elegance in African business discourse

Core Values

  • Depth over virality: Substance always trumps speed or shareability
  • African contextual relevance: Every piece is shaped by and for African economic, social, and cultural realities
  • Intellectual courage: Contrarian thinking is encouraged when supported by logic, evidence, or hard-won experience
  • Long-horizon focus: Prioritising generational impact over quarterly noise
  • Premium execution: Clean design, respectful tone, and high production standards that match our audience's calibre

  • Top articles consistently rated 4.7–5.0/5 by a discerning readership
